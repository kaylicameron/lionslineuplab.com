Statistical Modeling & Predictive Algorithms

The mathematical and machine learning foundation driving the LionsLineupLab forecast engine.

📐 Pythagorean Expectation

Adapted for the NFL, estimates a team's true fundamental winning probability from scoring differentials rather than noisy win-loss records.

Win % = (Points Scored)^2.37 / ((Points Scored)^2.37 + (Points Allowed)^2.37)

Teams significantly exceeding their Pythagorean win total frequently regress toward their fundamental expectation.

🌲 Gradient Boosting (XGBoost)

Ensemble tree-based algorithms excel at modeling non-linear interactions between variables like weather, rest days, and scheme mismatches without overfitting.

f(x) = sum(γ_m * h_m(x)) + λ * Ω(T)

Handles categorical team features, stadium types, and situational down-and-distance context effectively.

⚡ Expected Points Added (EPA)

Quantifies the point value of every individual play relative to historical field position, down, distance, and time remaining.

EPA = EP_(t+1) - EP_t

EPA per play is the gold standard for comparing offense and quarterback efficiency independent of volume.

🎲 Turnover Luck Regression

Fumble recoveries occur at approximately a 50% rate over large samples. Regression algorithms strip out lucky bounces to isolate true defensive takeaway skill.

True_TO = Forced_Fumbles * 0.50 + Adjusted_INTs

Teams that win primarily through outlier turnover margins regress toward the mean in future contests.
