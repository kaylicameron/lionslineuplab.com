📐 Pythagorean Expectation

Adapted for the NFL, calculates a team's true fundamental win probability based on point differentials rather than noisy single-score record variance.

Win % = (Points Scored)^2.37 / ((Points Scored)^2.37 + (Points Allowed)^2.37)

Teams outperforming their Pythagorean record naturally regress toward their fundamental efficiency over time.

🌲 Gradient Boosting (XGBoost)

Ensemble tree algorithms excel at capturing multi-variable non-linear interactions: QB efficiency under pressure, rest day mismatches, and weather.

f(x) = sum(γ_m * h_m(x)) + λ * Ω(T)

Provides high-accuracy spread predictions while remaining resistant to outliers.

⚡ Expected Points Added (EPA)

Assigns real point values to every snap based on historical down, distance, field location, and remaining time on the clock.

EPA = EP_(t+1) - EP_t

EPA/play is the gold standard metric for evaluating passing and running game efficiency independent of volume.

🎲 Turnover Luck Regression

Fumble recovery percentages hover around 50% across large game samples. Models isolate forced fumbles from lucky bounces to measure true takeaway strength.

Regressed_TO = Forced_Fumbles * 0.50 + Adjusted_INTs

Strips out fluke bounces to give a true baseline prediction for future performance.
